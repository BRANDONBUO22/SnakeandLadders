How to Play
Objective: Win the most levels across 3 levels to become champion!

How to Play:
1. Click ROLL on your turn
2. Your pawn moves forward automatically
3. Land on Ladder bottom → climb to top
4. Land on Snake head → slide down
5. Reach square 50 to complete the level
6. Player with most level wins is champion

Power-ups:
🛡️ Shield - Blocks next snake
🎲 2× Roll - Roll twice
⏭️ Skip Turn - Skip opponent's turn
⭐ Bonus Tiles - Land on 5·15·25·35·45 to earn power-ups
